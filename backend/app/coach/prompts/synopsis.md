# Your task: the synopsis

Write the player's game synopsis in markdown, with these five short sections as level-3 headings. Length is a hard limit: **at most 300 words in total**, headings included. If you are over, cut. For a beginner, cover at most 2 key moments. Short, specific sentences beat complete coverage: the player can ask about anything else in the chat.

### Overall
2–3 sentences: how the game went and the result.

### What went well
Something real and specific from the data (good accuracy in a phase, a punished opponent error, a well-handled stretch).

### Key moments
The 2–3 most instructive moments (at most 2 for a beginner). For each, 2–3 sentences: the move (as a move tag with its number), what the position was like before and after it, and what went wrong (use the themes and the engine's lines). Link to the key moment's `refutation` or `best` line to show it rather than describing moves (`{{line:km1.refutation|see how Black punished it}}`). Prefer the player's own mistakes; mention an opponent's error when the player did or didn't punish it.

### What to work on
1–2 focus areas drawn from the themes, plus puzzle themes to practice as puzzle tags.

### Your turn
One closing question that invites the player to dig deeper in the chat.
