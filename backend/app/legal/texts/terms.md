## The service

CoChess analyzes your chess games with an engine and explains what it finds through an AI coach. The coach's explanations are teaching aids: they can be wrong, and the engine's evaluations are the reference.

- If you are 13 to 17, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms for you, and any payment must be made by a parent or guardian.
- You sign in with your own Google account. Keep it secure; you're responsible for what happens under your account.

## Access

CoChess opens to new players a few at a time from a waitlist. The owner may pause an account that breaks these terms or misuses the service; you can still download or delete your data while it's paused.

## Credits and payments

- Coaching uses credits. New players get free trial credits. Analyzing games is free.
- You can subscribe for an allowance of credits each billing period, or buy extra credits. An allowance expires at the end of its billing period; bought and trial credits don't expire.
- Payments are handled by Stripe, which sells CoChess on our behalf and adds any sales tax at checkout. Refund requests go through Stripe.
- You can cancel a subscription at any time from your profile.

## Your content

Your games, notes and chats stay yours. You let CoChess process them to provide the service, including sending them to Anthropic's API to write your coaching (see the Privacy Policy). You can export or delete them at any time.
