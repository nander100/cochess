# Privacy Policy

**Draft for review.** This is a working draft that describes what CoChess does with your data today. It will be replaced by a version reviewed by a lawyer before CoChess opens to the public.

## Who we are

CoChess is a chess coaching app run by its owner. Questions about your data: reply to any CoChess email.

## What we collect

- **Your Google account's name and email**, when you sign in with Google. We use them to know who you are and to send you account emails.
- **Your age band**, not your birth year: whether you are 13 to 17 or 18 and over. We ask your birth year once and keep only the band.
- **Your games**: the games you upload or import, and what the engine finds in them.
- **What you tell the coach**: your chat messages, your answers in critical moments, and the notes in your profile (rating, time control, how you like to play).
- **Your Chess.com and Lichess usernames**, if you add them.
- **Your coaching credits**: what you were given, bought and spent.

## How we use it

- **Coaching.** To write your coaching, your games, chat messages and profile notes are sent to Anthropic's API (the company behind the Claude models). Anthropic processes them to produce the coach's answers.
- **Importing games.** Your Chess.com and Lichess usernames are used only to fetch your games from those sites.
- **Payments.** Payments are handled by Stripe, which sells CoChess subscriptions and credits on our behalf. Your card details go to Stripe, never to CoChess. Stripe keeps its own records of your payments.
- **Signing in.** Google tells us your name and email when you sign in.
- **Running the service.** The owner can see each account's name, email, status and usage (credits, coaching cost, number of games) to run and support CoChess. The owner doesn't read your games or chats in the dashboard.

## Cookies

CoChess sets one cookie: an essential login cookie that keeps you signed in. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

## Email

We send account emails only: your waitlist place, being accepted, changes to your access, your credits, changes to these terms, and confirmation that your account was deleted. You can turn off the credit emails in your profile or with the link in each one. We don't send newsletters or promotions.

## Your choices

- **Export.** Download everything that's yours (your profile, settings, games with your notes and variations, chats, reports and credit history) from your profile at any time.
- **Delete.** Delete your account from your profile. Your games, chats, reports and settings are deleted at once. Records of payments and coaching costs are kept without your name or account, because they are financial records; Stripe keeps its own payment records.

## Children

CoChess is a general-audience site and is not for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we delete it. Players aged 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian to agree to the terms and to make any payment.

## Changes

When this policy or the terms change, we'll ask you to review them the next time you sign in, and email you.
